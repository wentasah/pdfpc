
Problems:

- Running with just =-e HH:MM= doesn’t work

- Starting with =-t ST:RT -e HH:MM= before ST:RT works, but when one
  restarts with the same command line after ST:RT (e.g. after
  accidental close of pdfpc during the talk), timers are off by
  24 hours.

- Starting with =-t ST:RT -e EN:DT= is internally transformed to
  =-t ST:RT -d ENDT-STRT=. This is problematic when the the speaker
  accidentally pauses the timer (=p= key). After resuming, the
  count-down doesn’t show time until EN:DT, but until some later time,
  potentially causing the speaker to overrun the planned talk slot.
